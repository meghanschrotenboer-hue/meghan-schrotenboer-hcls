## Positioning against the field (working draft)
- **vs. IDMP software:** "Use the data platform you already pay for, and get AI-ready product data, not another silo."
- **vs. Big Four:** "Buyer-side operators who've sat in the seat, with incremental value you can see, not a roadmap deck."
- **vs. large SIs that straddle Pistoia and Snowflake (Accenture, Cognizant):** narrower and deeper: buyer-side HCLS experience, hands-on Snowflake delivery, and real fluency in PGO-T / IDMP-O. Lead with one concrete use case, not a transformation program ([A002](analyses/A002-pistoia-vs-snowflake-si-world.md)).
- **vs. consultancies in Pistoia:** "We land your standards in governed data products on the platform you already run, and measure the value." Narrower and more concrete than broad AI transformation.
- **vs. in-house build:** "Accelerators and maturity scaffolding that raise your project success rate" (the [S011](sources/S011-pistoia-nlp-llm-use-case-collection.md) evidence).
