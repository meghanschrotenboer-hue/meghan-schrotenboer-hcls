# S023 · Snowflake × Anthropic: $200M Agentic AI Partnership (Dec 2025)

**Why I saved it:** It's the first primary source linking a frontier lab to the platform at the center of the GTM thesis. Found during the gap audit ([A003](../analyses/A003-thesis-gap-audit.md)).

## TL;DR
- **Multi-year, $200M** expansion bringing **Claude** models into **Snowflake Cortex AI** for Snowflake's **12,600+ customers** across all three major clouds.
- Claude is a key model powering **Snowflake Intelligence**, Snowflake's enterprise intelligence agent.
- Includes a **joint global go-to-market initiative** focused on deploying AI agents. Before the expansion, thousands of Snowflake customers were already processing trillions of Claude tokens per month through Cortex.
