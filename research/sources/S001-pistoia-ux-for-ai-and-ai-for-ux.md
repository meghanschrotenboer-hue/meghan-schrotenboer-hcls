## TL;DR (provisional, from context only)
- Comes from Pistoia's **User Experience for Life Sciences (UXLS)** community, a precompetitive group of pharma UX leaders.
- The title's two-way framing ("UX *for* AI" and "AI *for* UX") matches UXLS's 2024 themes, *"How AI affects UXLS"* and *"How UXLS affects AI."*
- The community's public stance so far is **workflow first, AI second**: design the role AI plays before choosing the model.

## Our read (provisional)
**So what for services:** Pharma UX leaders are organizing precompetitively around *how* AI enters scientific workflows. A services firm that leads with workflow and role design, rather than a model or platform, speaks the language this community has already agreed on.

**So what for frontier labs:** The buyer-side UX community is signaling that "chat on top of everything" isn't the answer. Labs selling into life sciences will need workflow-embedded patterns and role clarity, which most will deliver through partners.

**Job to be done (UX lead):** When my company pushes AI into scientist-facing tools, I want a shared, vetted playbook for how AI should behave in a workflow, so I can avoid shipping AI that adds friction and erodes trust.
