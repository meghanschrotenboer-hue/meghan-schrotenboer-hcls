## TL;DR
- The cross-industry, not-for-profit **data management trade association**. It renamed itself the **EDM Association** in 2025 and **completed its acquisition of the Object Management Group's (OMG) assets on Oct 1, 2025**.
- It owns the frameworks CDOs already know: **DCAM** (data management capability maturity), **CDMC** (cloud data management controls), and **FIBO** (the financial industry ontology). It also supports the **IDMP ontology** for pharma.
- Its resources include a **Pharma / Life Sciences Global Data Management Benchmark Report**.

## What the source says
- **Identity:** "the leading non-profit, cross-industry data management and analytics trade association." ([About](https://edmcouncil.org/about/)) Member counts vary by release: 700+ member organizations and ~30,000 professionals post-merger; 350+ in a July 2025 release.
- **OMG combination:** announced July 1, 2025 ([press release](https://edmcouncil.org/announcement/edm-council-and-object-management-group-combine-to-create-global-end-to-end-data-and-standards-authority/)), completed Oct 1, 2025 ([completion](https://edmcouncil.org/announcement/edm-association-completes-acquisition-of-object-management-group-creating-worlds-largest-data-and-standards-community/)), framed as creating a "global end-to-end data and standards authority."
- **DCAM:** "the industry standard, best practices framework for data management and advanced analytics." DCAM v3 launched in 2025 per the About timeline. **[verify]**
- **CDMC:** a framework for cloud data management including **14 key automated controls** for protecting sensitive data in the cloud; available for organizations' internal use.
- **Ontologies:** FIBO plus others; the **IDMP ontology** is listed as serving pharma and healthcare. Its stated aim matches Pistoia's: "deep, semantic interoperability based on FAIR principles."
- **AI framing:** leadership positions the association's work around **AI-ready data**.

## Our read

**The standards stack is consolidating, and IDMP-O sits on it.** IDMP-O imports the OMG Commons Ontology Library and plans to reach ISO through OMG's PAS process ([S002](S002-pistoia-idmp-o-github.md)). Its project wiki originated on EDM Council infrastructure. With OMG now inside the EDM Association, one body spans enterprise data-management maturity (DCAM), cloud controls (CDMC), and the ontology standards path pharma relies on. For anyone selling AI-ready data in life sciences, that's a stakeholder map worth knowing.

**DCAM and the FAIR Maturity Matrix are complements, not competitors.** The FAIR Maturity Matrix ([S007](S007-fair-maturity-matrix.md)) cites DCAM as an "industrial maturity model for data and its governance." DCAM is the language enterprise CDOs (often recruited from financial services) already speak. The FAIR matrix is the life-sciences-specific, R&D-friendly lens. A services firm fluent in both can translate between the CDO office and R&D.

**CDMC is a natural bridge to cloud data platforms.** Cloud data controls are table stakes for any AI workload touching regulated or sensitive data. **[link to platform-specific CDMC alignment if relevant to your practice]**

**So what for services:** Pair DCAM (enterprise governance) + FAIR MM (R&D data) + UXLS (adoption) into a single readiness view. Each is buyer-recognized, so the assessment's credibility doesn't rest on your own proprietary framework.

**So what for frontier labs:** Enterprise data leaders increasingly evaluate AI readiness through these frameworks. Labs that map their enterprise controls to CDMC-style expectations will shorten security and governance reviews.

**Job to be done (enterprise CDO):** When AI raises the stakes on data quality and control, I want to benchmark our data management against a recognized industry standard, so I can prioritize investment and defend it to the board and regulators.

| Current state | Future state | Gap (the offer) |
|---|---|---|
| Data governance maturity unmeasured or measured with a homegrown model | Benchmarked against DCAM; R&D benchmarked against FAIR MM | Combined DCAM × FAIR × UXLS assessment |
| Cloud controls inconsistent across AI workloads | CDMC-aligned controls applied to AI data pipelines | Cloud data controls for AI |
