# S018 · Pistoia London 2026 Conference Poll (April 2026)

**Why I saved it:** Found while researching [A001](../analyses/A001-evolv-pistoia-snowflake-si.md). It's strong T8 evidence, and it shows a consultancy (Thoughtworks) using Pistoia as a thought-leadership channel.

> ⚠️ **Partial read.** Figures come from Pistoia's press release and trade coverage via search. The sample is described differently by source: attendees of a ~300-person event (press release) vs. "170 pharmaceutical R&D leaders" (Thoughtworks).

## TL;DR
- **30%** have an enterprise-wide AI platform, yet **69% lack clear metrics** for AI's impact on R&D cost or time, and **just 4%** report tangible AI benefits for R&D leadership.
- **59%** say their companies must prioritize **data quality and accessibility**; **22%** point to adoption and change management.
- **Regulatory submission and reporting teams benefit most (54%)**; only **1%** see value in the wet lab.

## What the source says
- Enterprise-wide AI platform: 30%. No clear metrics for AI's impact on R&D cost or time: **69%**. Tangible benefits for R&D leadership: **4%**. ([R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/new-pistoia-alliance-survey-shows-just-1-of-professionals-report-ais-value-in-the-wet-lab/); [AZoLifeSciences](https://www.azolifesciences.com/news/20260429/New-Pistoia-Alliance-Poll-Finds-Regulatory-Teams-Benefit-Most-from-AI-But-Just-1-25-See-Value-in-the-Wet-Lab.aspx))
- Pistoia's president: "AI success depends on getting both the data and people elements right." 59% prioritize data quality and accessibility; 22% adoption and change management.
- Where AI helps most: regulatory submissions and reporting 54%; research analysis 21%; automating scientific workflows and experiments 13%; wet lab 1%.
- **Sponsor:** the poll was sponsored by **Thoughtworks**, which published a companion e-book, [From pilots to platforms: AI in pharma](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/e-books/from-pilots-to-platforms-ai-in-pharma).

## Our read

**The strongest single statistic for T8 so far: 69% can't measure AI's impact.** Pair it with S011 (57% of projects can't state their business outcome) and the value-measurement gap is now confirmed by two independent Pistoia instruments.

**Regulatory is where AI value shows up first.** 54% name regulatory submission and reporting teams, consistent with S011 (regulatory projects most mature) and T4. That's where to lead.

**Data and people, not models.** 59% data quality plus 22% adoption and change lines up with T1 and T6. Nobody named model capability as the priority.

**Thoughtworks shows the play.** A consultancy sponsored a Pistoia poll, put its name next to the headline numbers in trade press, and published an e-book from it. That's a repeatable, affordable way for a services firm to earn visibility with Pistoia's 200+ members. It's directly relevant to [A001](../analyses/A001-evolv-pistoia-snowflake-si.md).

**So what for services:** Two moves. (1) Lead with **AI value measurement** (69%) and **regulatory reporting** (54%) offers. (2) Consider sponsoring or co-designing a Pistoia poll at a future conference on a topic you want to own (e.g., trustworthy clinical data for AI).

**So what for frontier labs:** Pharma R&D leaders aren't seeing AI value in the lab yet (1%) and can't measure it elsewhere. Labs that help customers measure impact will hold their accounts.
