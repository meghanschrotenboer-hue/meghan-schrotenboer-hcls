## Our read

**The intersection is real, and it's already occupied by large SIs.** Accenture and Cognizant each hold both seats: Pistoia membership and top Snowflake partner status. That proves the worlds aren't mutually exclusive. It also means a boutique Snowflake SI isn't first to the intersection. It has to be **more specific**: HCLS buyer-side depth plus fluency in the actual standards (PGO-T, IDMP-O, FAIR).

**Data vendors are the connective tissue.** IQVIA and Benchling are both Pistoia participants *and* Snowflake data-sharing partners. Their data arrives in customers' Snowflake accounts, which is exactly where standards-based semantics need to be applied.

**Snowflake's absence from Pistoia is a structural fact, not an accident.** Pistoia is vendor-neutral and pharma-led. Platforms reach it through members (SIs, data vendors, pharma) rather than directly. That's the role an SI plays.
